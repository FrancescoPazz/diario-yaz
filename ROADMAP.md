# Roadmap

Ideas for the next changes, most useful first. Each one should be a small, reviewable change.

1. **Tests for the calendar map around a skipped placebo** — `buildMap` when a skipped pack is followed by a late pack
   (gap days, skipped period marker on a gap day).
2. **Accessibility pass, part three** — check contrast of the legend's small dots, focus order in the Add/Edit dialog
   and richer TalkBack labels on the Today pack (active or placebo, pills left).
3. **Shrink the FAB while scrolling** — shrink the extended "Add" FAB to its icon-only form when the History list is
   scrolled down, and expand it again at the top, as in Material 3.
4. **Scroll to the first flagged pack** — when History is opened from the Today overlap hint, scroll to and focus the
   first overlapping row.
5. **Calendar legend in dark mode** — review the legend and the day-detail sheet in dark mode for contrast and
   spacing on small screens.
6. **Pack count in History** — show the same "pack N since ..." summary at the top of History, with the number of
   skipped periods.
